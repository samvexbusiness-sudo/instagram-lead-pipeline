# Kallaway Playbook (for Sam)

Built from 5 note files covering 43 Kallaway videos (`notes/`, list in `SOURCES.md`). Numbers are only ones Kallaway states, with the video id in brackets. They're his claims, not facts we checked. "Coach" = Sam's client type: a solo sales/marketing/content/client-acquisition coach with a paid offer, posting talking-head reels for leads.

---

## 1. The 10 rules that matter most (ranked for a coach's reels)

1. **Every reel is about one buyer pain that the paid offer solves.** Why: "if your content isn't cutting through, it is always an idea problem" [tKRDZqXgLcQ]. Off-offer topics grow followers who never buy ("trust dilution") [1ilMGCxJBQY].
2. **Every reel ends with one ramp matched to its topic** (comment keyword → freebie on the same topic → offer). Why: signal × trust × ramp × offer multiply, so if any of them is zero you get zero sales [pSIY9ngEZC8]. Viewers don't remember you between reels, so each one has to work alone [ceRZVxO8KF8].
3. **Line one gives subject + stakes as a flat statement in "you" language. No warm-up, no "if you want X…".** Why: viewers give you about 4 seconds [LmXpbP7dD48]. On his own account "if-then" hooks flopped and declarative ones crushed [TNEyZxihwno].
4. **Proof comes in line 2 or 3, on screen.** Why: right after the hook the viewer checks "can I trust this?" [6G1wIdxWF5w]. "Tease the proof" is lock-in method #1 [vmsDhY9ITlI].
5. **Hook the tactical pain, not "make more money".** Why: core-pain hooks set off the viewer's BS detector. His money videos flop while specific how-to videos crush [g_6TIu7zHDw].
6. **The text hook is the most important part of the hook, and it says the same thing as the spoken line.** Why: eyes hunt for text first. Talking heads ("yappers") win on text + spoken alone [pNIYikmYsyw].
7. **Say the common belief, then the coach's real opposite take.** Why: "if your opinions are the same as everyone else, you are just a derivative… you'll always lose" [S-7VkwSU0gs]. Contrast creates the curiosity.
8. **Stay on one topic and one avatar. Use broad topics only if they stay inside the buyer's world (3-2-1 mix).** Why: one viral hit outside the core audience gives the next several videos bad sample data [8cQidXgtGmU]. Making every video ultra-narrow also underperforms [94ezZMYPzls].
9. **Judge reels by leads per view, in batches, not by views.** Why: for authority builders "dollars per view" is the only fair comparison [o_n5ED5fE1I]. In a niche, 10K targeted views is "extremely good" [94ezZMYPzls].
10. **Get volume from a system: 1 to 2 formats repeated like a TV show, with batched scripts and recording.** Why: low volume is a systems problem, and editing is the main thing keeping owners at 2 to 4 videos a week [viT-_SjCs5I, TNEyZxihwno]. Viewers don't get bored of a format; creators do [w-RgtfUnYVo].

## 2. Client strategy, step by step

### Step A: Positioning (one 60 to 90 min call, then a one-page file per client)
Order: offer first, then content. "You can make content work for almost any offer, but not any offer for any audience" [bqzd0h0gmU0].

**Fill-in: `CLIENT-<name>.md`** (Kallaway's niche interviewer questions [vwlK6MbdAto])
- What they sell, at what price, who pays: ___
- The chair test, one buyer in a chair (role, stage, revenue, beliefs): ___
- Dream outcome in the buyer's words: ___
- Top 5 pains in the buyer's words (from sales calls and DMs): ___
- Target authority statement: "I help ___ with ___ in ___ way" [S-7VkwSU0gs]
- Unfair advantage / real proof (client results, own numbers, screenshots available?): ___
- Bullseye, 5 rings from exact buyer out to broad [S-7VkwSU0gs, vwlK6MbdAto]. Example: "sales coaches selling $3K+ programs" → "coaches" → "service businesses" → "sales" → "business". ___
- Contrarian vault, 3 to 5 big beliefs where the coach disagrees with the niche [S-7VkwSU0gs]. "Everyone says ___, I believe ___ because ___"
- 1 to 3 terms to own in the viewer's head, plus the method name [S-7VkwSU0gs]: ___
- Points of uniqueness vs 5 to 10 competitors on 5 dimensions: topic, substance, avatar, format, visuals. Where is the white space? [S-7VkwSU0gs]
- Offer filter for every topic: "Does watching this get the buyer one step closer to the outcome the offer sells?" No = don't make it [ceRZVxO8KF8].

### Step B: Research and ideas (every 2 weeks)
- Watchlist of 20 to 25 coach accounts at 10K to 250K followers, last 3 to 6 months, engagement rate of at least 2% (filters out paid posts), sorted by outlier score, top 50 to 100 analysed [TNEyZxihwno, 1ilMGCxJBQY].
- Cluster them into buckets. Pick 1 broad and 2 narrow buckets for the 3-2-1 mix (below).
- For each candidate ask: "do I believe something different than they said?" Skip it if not [TNEyZxihwno].
- **Idea = topic (from data) + angle (from the coach) + substance.** The angle question: "What's the one or two things I give my clients on this that they're happiest about?" [tKRDZqXgLcQ]
- **Shock test:** out of 100 buyers, how many have NOT heard this? Keep the highest-scoring true points. If nothing scores, don't film it [_Z11mjFh2zY].
- **Boring/old topic fix (illusion of novelty)** [LvuoNlYRs7g]: show a new angle or name on the old thing + tie it to an outcome + put it next to the belief they hold + proof. Template: "Everyone thinks X, but actually it's Y, if you want Z." The new *name* is fine, but the claim, numbers, proof and any urgency must be real.
- Pick the angle "one click above" the buyer's level (a 2-3/10 buyer gets 4-5/10 content) [94ezZMYPzls].

**Fill-in: idea card**
`Topic (3-5 words): ___ | Bucket: broad / narrow B / narrow C / chaos | Common belief: ___ | Coach's take: ___ | Proof available: ___ | Shock score guess: __/100 | Source outlier link: ___`

### Step C: Hooks
**Spoken hook: 4 S's.** Subject in sentence one, Stakes (the one most often missing), Simple words, Super clear [tKRDZqXgLcQ]. Diagnose in order: Delay → Confusion → Irrelevance → Disinterest [2byPP_9F0-Q].

Kallaway formulas, set up for coaches:
- **Declarative contrast (his best rewrite):** "Most [coaches] do [A]. The ones who [outcome] do [B]." His version: "Most creators trigger one type of dopamine in their videos. The addictive ones use a six-level ladder." [a7VjpIqq8Xk]
- **Lean → interjection → snapback** [LmXpbP7dD48]: "[Topic + pain]. [Belief they hold]. But get this, [opposite], because [real answer]." B has to actually pay off, or "you're a fraud".
- **Bull's-eye opener** [pSIY9ngEZC8]: line 1 topic, line 2 pain, line 3 teased named solution.
- **Problem hook:** "If your [specific symptom], it's [this one thing]." Use a symptom they've literally seen ("calls end in 'let me think about it'"), not "struggling with sales".
- **Case study hook:** "[Client] went from [real A] to [real B] using [named method]." [_Z11mjFh2zY]
- **Negative flip** [pcnrzBwoVUk]: "Here's how to X" → "This is killing your X" / "This [mistake] is costing you [outcome]."
- Hook format → story structure pairs [_Z11mjFh2zY]: Problem → problem-solver; Contrarian → breakdown; Case study → case-study explainer; Personal experience → personal story; List → listicle.
- Power words: pull the phrases that repeat in the coach's own top hooks and competitors' top hooks, then twist them with synonyms. Don't copy them [pNIYikmYsyw].

**Text hook** [pNIYikmYsyw]: subject repeater + ONE of pain / outcome / state change. Max 2 lines, break at the phrase, no and/or/that/but/therefore, no punctuation except quotes and parentheses, specific numbers only if they're real, on screen about 3 s, about a third down, never over the eyes or mouth. Lead with the plain category, not an unfamiliar product name. Examples: `DISCOVERY CALLS / THAT GO NOWHERE`, `YOUR OFFER PAGE / IS LOSING BUYERS`.

**Caption, first line:** restate the hook in plain words. It "subliminally" affects whether people stay [pNIYikmYsyw].

**Lock-in zone = the 1 to 3 sentences after the hook.** Pick one of six [vmsDhY9ITlI]:
| Situation | Method | Coach line shape |
|---|---|---|
| Real numbers exist | Tease the proof | "One client changed this line and [real result]." |
| No numbers, obvious change | Tease the transformation | "…and calls stop feeling like begging." |
| Premise is kind of mid | Rapid contrast | "You were taught [old way]. The problem is [why it fails]. So instead…" |
| Coach has a method | Magic box (name it, explain later; stacks with all) | "I call it the [Name]. It's 3 steps." |
| Obvious objection | Objection handling | "And no, this isn't another script to memorise." |
| Claim is new or unclear | Bold claim double-down | Say the key claim again in other words. |

**Hook checklist (tick all):** ☐ topic in sentence 1 ☐ "you" not "I" ☐ need-to-have pain, not nice-to-have ☐ statement, not question ☐ contrast present ☐ one reading only ☐ text = spoken meaning ☐ lock-in method chosen ☐ claim matches what the body delivers.

### Step D: Script structure (30 to 60 s talking head)
1. **Hook (0 to 3 s):** WHAT + WHY first; WHO/HOW (credibility) second; WHEN/WHERE last or cut. "Lead with the cake, not the icing" [WluuZB_ck9Q].
2. **Lock-in:** proof or named method (table above).
3. **Common belief out loud → contrarian take → named mechanism** [7I50PECz7SU].
4. **Point 1 = second-best point.** Then the best one, then the rest. If you lead with the best, viewers learn that value is dropping [7I50PECz7SU].
5. **Each point = value loop:** what it is → how (one real example) → why it matters [7I50PECz7SU].
6. **Loop opener between points** with a contrast word: "But that only works if you also fix this." [pcnrzBwoVUk]
7. **Aha #2 → CTA right after** (the reciprocity peak) [W--tE9w_N_A].
8. **Last line:** the quotable rule that loops back to line one. Write it first [t5Z-Q1bg1tU].

**Fill-in reel template**
```
HOOK: Most [buyer] [do A]. The ones who [outcome] [do B].
TEXT HOOK: [SUBJECT] / [PAIN or OUTCOME]
LOCK-IN: [proof line | named method | objection]
BELIEF: You've been told [A].
TAKE: Turns out [B]. I call it the [Name].
POINT 1 (2nd best): [what] → [real example] → [why it matters]
LOOP: But that only works if you also [X].
POINT 2 (best, aha #2): [what] → [example] → [why]
CTA: Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you the [Name] [thing], it's for [who].
LAST LINE: [rule that loops to the hook]
```
**Language pass:** cut if/maybe/might/probably/could. Use "when you do this" and "the reason this works" [pcnrzBwoVUk]. Every beat is joined by but/therefore. Jagged line lengths. "Gossip whisperer, not town crier" [LvuoNlYRs7g].
**Final 4 questions** [_Z11mjFh2zY]: Interesting? As compressed as possible? Does the hook hook me on its own? What emotion do I feel at the end?

### Step E: Storytelling devices (for client-win or own-failure reels)
- **Stakes = who + what's at risk + why NOW.** Urgency multiplies it ("the call was in 2 hours") [KyC8r-zitVE].
- **Big question with enough detail to guess:** "Within 30 minutes she told me something that changed my plan for the next 6 months," not "the call went well" [KyC8r-zitVE].
- **Head fake:** work out what the viewer predicts, then reveal something that contrasts with it and makes sense right away (clues planted) [KyC8r-zitVE].
- **Re-hook with no gap:** "which would've been great, except…", "and that's when I realised…" [KyC8r-zitVE].
- **Villain:** "they do this, we do that." It can be a group or an old method [WluuZB_ck9Q].
- **Thought narration**, once per reel: "Now you're probably thinking, my leads are different." [pcnrzBwoVUk]
- **Conversion story, 5 beats** [W--tE9w_N_A]: pain + emotion → how the coach failed with the usual method → tease the new named method → real result → CTA. Only with real results.
- **Fear:** show the fear happening (an empty calendar on screen) instead of saying "you'll fail". Tease the fix early. Short form needs to be more explicit about it [IywTdYKncqY].

### Step F: Editing and visuals (what the EDITOR controls)
- **First second:** a small move is enough: lean-in, punch-in on the first word, text sliding in. Overdoing it looks gimmicky [pNIYikmYsyw].
- **Text hook design** to the rules in Step C. It's a separate system-font line, not just the word-for-word captions.
- **Triple alignment:** check the frame muted. Do text, visual and spoken say one thing? [pNIYikmYsyw]
- **Proof flashes:** 1 to 2 real artifacts (client DM, Stripe, booked calendar) flashed fast right after the claim [ZM3elcBE48I].
- **Cut every throat-clear before the topic.** If the what/why line exists later in the take, move it to the top [WluuZB_ck9Q].
- **Reorder points** so the second-best comes first (works only if points stand alone).
- **Kill dead air at section ends** ("anyways…", "so yeah"). Close one loop and open the next back to back [KyC8r-zitVE].
- **Paint the picture:** B-roll, screen recording or a simple graphic wherever a process is only told in words [WluuZB_ck9Q].
- **End on the strongest line**, not "hope that helps".
- **CTA:** keyword on screen + in the caption + tied to the topic (skill s13).
- **Signature look per client** across 6 dimensions: character, set, visual selection, layout, typography, motion. AI-edited reels are converging on "the same look and feel" [tKRDZqXgLcQ]. Polish counts when it contrasts with the niche; noisy retention tricks that bury the value don't [I4bES-sGzdM, 46t_cn0lx2E].
- **Trust signals in the format:** real desk/office, clean image, dialled audio [94ezZMYPzls].
- **Recording direction (send before the shoot)** [ZM3elcBE48I]: a few throwaway takes first (the intro is where people are coldest); line by line; falling tone at sentence ends (upspeak kills authority); a half-second pause after the key word; hold energy through the last word ("you can't give yourself more energy in the edit"); if Sam wrote it, the coach spends 10 to 20 min putting it in their own words.

### Step G: CTA and sales from content
Four switches, all non-zero [pSIY9ngEZC8]:
1. **Bull's-eye signal:** the viewer thinks "that's exactly my problem." Otherwise the reel gets filed as entertainment.
2. **Trust:** stack proof. Strongest to weakest: warm referral > audience size > competence shown in the reel > group testimonials with names and numbers > own results > borrowed proof > cold testimonial > third-party stat. More pain needs less trust.
3. **Ramp:** lead magnet (default, lowest friction), sales page (low ticket or high trust), call/DM (high ticket, lower trust). One per reel, matched to the topic ("don't talk about New York for 8 hours then offer an exit to Tulsa").
4. **Aligned offer:** the message in the content matches the offer.

- Not a sales ask 9 times out of 10. Trade more help for an email [ceRZVxO8KF8].
- **Native embed:** at outline stage, pick the point the freebie naturally solves and offer it there [7I50PECz7SU].
- Never press Boost on reels (F tier, "will nuke your channel"). Ads Manager to a landing page is fine [SJ0OtNuUzls].
- His benchmark: about 1% of viewers gave an email [TNEyZxihwno]. His CTA testing: 8.3 → 31.3 leads per 1,000 views from 4 attributes he doesn't reveal [gEdt3AGLWjI].

**Fill-in CTA:** `Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [exact thing] so you can [outcome of THIS reel]. It's for [who].`

### Step H: Cadence and volume
- One home platform (Instagram for most, because of the ManyChat ecosystem) + email, for 6 months [ceRZVxO8KF8, h_yQswsXwhY].
- Ramp: 3 videos week 1, 5 in week 2, then 7 a week for 6 weeks (about 50 videos), then double down and cut [S-7VkwSU0gs].
- **3-2-1 week** [1ilMGCxJBQY]: 2 broad-within-niche + 2 narrow bucket B + 2 narrow bucket C + 1 chaos agent (a triple-down or a format from another niche, never fully off-niche).
- Content formula = topic + format + visual layout. Aim for 5 to 10 that work [S-7VkwSU0gs].
- Coach's time: batch 7 to 10 scripts in 3 to 4 h, record 7 to 10 in about an hour, Sam edits [TNEyZxihwno, tKRDZqXgLcQ].
- Simple formats a busy owner can shoot fast: scenarios, ranking, tier list, three levels [bqzd0h0gmU0].
- Patience: "1 year to get average, 3 years to get good" [bqzd0h0gmU0].

### Step I: Measure and iterate
- Metrics in order: leads per view (ManyChat), then followers per view, then views relative to the batch [B9l9TRhu5Vw].
- **Batch of 10 rules** [B9l9TRhu5Vw]: nothing at 5x+ = redo research (something obvious is wrong); 5 to 10x = tweak topic or format for 3 of the next 10; 10x+ = same topic AND format for 3 of the next 10, never all 10.
- Every 2 weeks: kill a bucket if all 4 of its videos flop, and promote a new one [1ilMGCxJBQY].
- Judge each formula on 4 things [S-7VkwSU0gs]: hits the metric? attracts the buyer (comments, DMs, new followers' bios)? coach enjoys it? proud of it?
- **$1,000 trust test:** a buyer-type person watches, then says what problem it solves and whether they'd pay $1,000 to fix it. If they can't say, the message is unclear [viT-_SjCs5I].
- "It worked 3 to 6 months ago and stopped" = the niche's quality bar rose. Raise idea and execution; don't hunt for hacks [w-RgtfUnYVo].
- Ignore posting time, hashtags and settings hacks [SJ0OtNuUzls].

**Fill-in batch report (1 page):** `Batch # | top 3 by leads/view | bottom 3 | what top 3 share (topic/format/hook/lock-in/CTA) | ONE brick to test next 10 | buckets kept/killed | 3 of next 10 = ___`

## 3. Diagnose a prospect's reel in 60 seconds

Go in order. Stop at the first big failure, note the timestamp, then fix that one thing in the mockup.

| # | Check (how) | Broken looks like | Edit fixes? | Script fix? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mute 0 to 3 s.** Can a stranger tell the topic? | Captions only, no text hook, static face | ✅ add text hook + small move | — |
| 2 | **Delay:** when is the topic first named? | "So…", "Okay guys", "Let me tell you a story", topic at 0:04+ | ✅ if the line exists later: cut the warm-up | ✅ if no what/why line exists |
| 3 | **"I" vs "you"** in the first 2 sentences | "I've spent years…" | ❌ | ✅ |
| 4 | **Statement or question?** | "Do you struggle with…?" / "If you want X…" | ❌ | ✅ |
| 5 | **Contrast?** Does it say something the viewer doesn't already believe? | Generic tip every coach says | ❌ | ✅ |
| 6 | **Alignment:** text = spoken = visual? | Text says something else, or > 2 lines, covers face, gone before ~3 s | ✅ rewrite text hook | — |
| 7 | **Core-pain hook?** | "6 figures", "more clients" head-on | partly (text hook can name the tactical pain) | ✅ |
| 8 | **Lock-in (3 to 10 s):** proof / named method / objection? | Jumps to body or backstory, proof only at the end | ✅ if proof exists: screenshot on screen right after the hook | ✅ if none |
| 9 | **Dead air / "and then"** at section ends | "Anyways…", flat list | ✅ cut gaps, add a text tease | ✅ spoken loop openers |
| 10 | **Ending** | "Hope that helps", "follow for more" | ✅ end on the strongest line | ✅ if no quotable line |
| 11 | **Ramp:** keyword on screen + in caption + same topic as reel? Freebie leads toward the offer? | No CTA, generic CTA, freebie on another topic | partly (show keyword on screen, move CTA after the best point) | ✅ if no matching keyword/freebie |
| 12 | **Caption line 1** | "🔥 Big one today" | ✅ (copy) | — |
| 13 | **Profile:** pinned reels on-offer? Topics drifting from the offer? | Pinned = old/off-offer; lifestyle/mindset reels | ❌ (strategy) | ✅ (topic choice) |

**Mockup rule:** if it's an edit fix, mock it up (new text hook, cut warm-up, proof flash). If it's a script fix, show a rewritten first 3 lines.

**Phrase the business effect: their buyers, leads and money, not "better edits".** Shapes:
- Delay: "Your topic only shows up at 0:04, so most of the coaches you could help are gone before they hear what you fix."
- No text hook: "With the sound off, nobody can tell this is about [topic], so it gets sorted as entertainment, not a solution."
- Proof late: "You have client results, but they show up at 0:40. Trust gets decided in the first few seconds, so the people who'd buy never see them."
- No ramp: "This reel warms people up, then gives them nowhere to go, so none of those views can turn into a lead."
- Misaligned freebie: "This reel is about [X], the freebie is about [Y], so the people you just warmed up have nothing matching to grab."
- Topic drift: "Your most-viewed reels are on topics your coaching doesn't sell, so they grow followers who won't buy."
- Never promise leads or calls. Say only what's visible (skill s4, s13).

## 4. Sam's own account (only what Kallaway's material supports)

Honest limit: **no video covers accounts getting under ~200 views or with under 10 followers.** The closest he says is that a bad first sample of about 200 people makes the platform "stop pushing almost immediately" [8cQidXgtGmU]. Don't invent a reason beyond that. He also says nothing about editor portfolios or before/after formats.

What his rules point to (applied by us, not said about Sam):
1. **Pick the game: authority building, buyer = the coach.** Judge posts by replies/leads per view, not views [o_n5ED5fE1I]. One platform, one avatar, one offer ("one by one by one") [fVsl4ZKCPik].
2. **A before/after is a format, not a topic.** It needs a coach pain + angle + substance. "Why your reels don't get leads" fits. A reel that just shows nicer cuts fails his four tests: relevant, non-obvious, understood, quick to apply [8cQidXgtGmU]. The plan (unique prospect footage + a hook that names the viewer) fits his "bull's-eye signaling" and "audience of one" [pSIY9ngEZC8, DFSU-YPRTs8].
3. **Say the diagnosis out loud.** The platform classifies mostly from the transcript, then somewhat from the caption [h_yQswsXwhY]. A wordless before/after gives it little to match (our inference). Spoken line + text hook should name the coach's problem.
4. **Stay on one topic for reps.** 30 to 50 videos on one pain-point topic to "train" it [h_yQswsXwhY]. Expect 1 to 3 batches of 10 to find a first 10x outlier [o_n5ED5fE1I].
5. **Phase 1 = one at a time.** For the first 20 to 30 videos: make, post, analyse, then make the next. Don't batch at this stage [DFSU-YPRTs8].
6. **CTA on every post**, matched to the post (for example a keyword for a free hook rewrite) [ceRZVxO8KF8]. No Boost [SJ0OtNuUzls]. Ignore hashtags and posting time.
7. **Profile = the next sale.** Under 10K followers "people typically pass" unless the pinned videos and next 2 to 3 reels convince them [6G1wIdxWF5w]. Pin the 3 strongest before/afters, build a binge bank.
8. **Angles he hands an editor:** (a) AI editing is making a "sea of sameness", so human taste and a signature style are the moat [tKRDZqXgLcQ]; (b) editing is the #1 bottleneck keeping owners at 2 to 4 posts a week instead of 7 [TNEyZxihwno]; (c) a fair editor price is $50 to $150 per video, and below that "you get less quality and taste" [SJ0OtNuUzls].
9. **Proof caution while small:** he held back case-study claims while small because it felt "incongruent" [1ilMGCxJBQY]. Show the before/after; don't claim results for the prospect.
10. **Don't:** chase broad editor-audience views (wrong buyer) [94ezZMYPzls], post lifestyle "you are the niche" content [DFSU-YPRTs8], or copy viral editor reels with 80% kept [w-RgtfUnYVo].

Not from Kallaway, but check it: get the prospect's OK before posting their footage publicly.

## 5. AI/Claude workflow gaps worth building (ranked)

Kallaway runs these on Sandcastles (his paid data tool). Sam's would run on vidIQ / Apify / transcripts. His rule: without real performance data, AI "is just guessing" [vwlK6MbdAto].
1. **Client intake → `CLIENT-<name>.md`** (the Step A file from a call transcript). Every other skill reads it.
2. **Batch report per client, scheduled:** pull new reels, rank by leads/view, apply the 5x/10x rules, name ONE brick to test. This is the retainer glue.
3. **Topic Scanner:** his top-100 prompt (in `notes/notes_ai.md`) over the client's watchlist → 3 to 5 word topics + a pitch against the TAS, 8 to 15 buckets ranked by outlier score.
4. **Contrarian vault:** extract claims competitors repeat, ask the coach "agree or not?", store the answers. AI can't invent the take [B9l9TRhu5Vw].
5. **Client hook grader:** hooks of client + niche winners → format-matched Mad Libs + a rubric merged with sam-content-brain s3 → grade Sam's draft and suggest 3 rewrites [a7VjpIqq8Xk].
6. **CTA analyser:** tag each reel's spoken CTA vs leads per 1,000 views (needs ManyChat data from the client).
7. **Bullseye plot:** client's last 25+ reels by ring and bucket, with views (+ leads if shared) → a 3-2-1 week plan.
8. **Breakout/eras on a prospect:** find the spike reels and what changed, as Loom material.
Not worth building: one-shot script writing (his ceiling is 80 to 90%, with a human finishing it [vwlK6MbdAto]) and AI editing ("anti-slop" [tKRDZqXgLcQ]).
