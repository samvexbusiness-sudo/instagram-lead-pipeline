**Top takeaways**
1. The idea is the bottleneck. Every reel = one buyer pain the paid offer solves. Off-offer topics grow followers who won't buy.
2. Every reel needs a ramp (keyword → freebie on the same topic → offer). His funnel is signal × trust × ramp × offer: any zero = zero sales.
3. Hook: topic + stakes in sentence one, flat statement, "you" language. Declaratives beat "if you want X…" on his own data. Hook the tactical pain (the discovery call), not "make more money".
4. Text hook is the most important hook part for talking heads (text > spoken > visual). It must match the spoken line. The caption's first line is a 4th hook.
5. The 1 to 3 sentences after the hook (the "lock-in") decide retention. There are six methods; for coaches the default is tease the proof, then name the method.
6. Script: what + why first, second-best point first, loop openers with no dead air, CTA right after the second "aha", last line loops to the hook.
7. Strategy: 3-2-1 weekly mix (broad inside the buyer's world + 2 narrow buckets + 1 chaos), outlier research on 10K to 250K accounts with a 2% engagement floor, judge by leads per view in batches of 10.
8. Editor's 2026 lever: a signature visual style per client, because AI edits are converging on one look. Polish that adds clarity, not noise.
9. Own account: he never covers sub-200-view accounts. His rules point to: buyer = coach, the problem said out loud in every post (the platform reads the transcript), one topic for 30 to 50 posts, pinned reels as the sales page.
