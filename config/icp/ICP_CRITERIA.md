# ICP criteria

## Current ICP (Sam, 2026-10-30 — this wins over everything below)

A business coach who talks about **sales, marketing, content, client acquisition** (leadership is fine too)
and gives **tactical value**, not mindset, feelings, emotions, manifesting or spirituality.

- **Already understands content as a lead-generation machine.** They post talking-head or similar content
  with a clear CTA (DM keyword, lead magnet, call booking, application link).
- **Has footage**: talking-head reels, podcast, long-form or anything that can be recut.
- **Active**: posting recently and regularly.
- **Targetable size**: not so big they'll never see an email (roughly under 40K followers),
  not so small they're broke (tiny accounts with no visible offer are out). A blue check is fine.
- **Solo**: the coach is the face and the business. Not an agency, not a company brand with a big team.
  A small team is fine, as long as they don't already have people doing their content.
- **Sells coaching, programs, masterminds or info** to business owners and professionals.
  Not software, not content/marketing/social media agencies (those are competitors).
- **Email is the outreach channel**, so a lead without a findable business email can't be used yet.

Tier A = all of the above clearly visible. Tier B = fits, with one open question.
Ambiguity means Out.

## Older criteria (from the sam-freelance-business skill, kept for reference)

**Niche, decided on WHO not on format.** Business coaches who sell to founders and professionals: content that turns into customers, paid or organic. Business coaches with an offer are fine, pure marketing coaches are scarce and many are competitors. Format follows the client. "Talking head editor" is a format, not a niche.

### The 60-second triage (three kill checks, ambiguity means Out)
1. **Instagram exists, under about 40K followers, no checkmark.** Over that or verified, their inbox buries a DM.
2. **Raw or lightly edited scripted talking head.** Ideal: list reels ("5 biggest mistakes founders make") where the edit is just captions. If they already have a polished repeatable style, the edit shows no upgrade. Skip raw emotional monologues, letterboxed long-form clips, green screen and screen recordings.
3. **Sells coaching, info or ads to experts.** Not software, not content services (they are competitors or have in-house teams).

A prospect needs an Instagram OR an email. **Email is the last check, not a gate.**

### Then the source-quality screen (before spending hours)
- Can one real hook be found in a few minutes of skimming the transcript? If not, scrap the prospect or the video.
- Is the footage usable (resolution, audio, framing)?
- **Double-duty filter:** the default pick shoots dedicated camera-facing long-form or clean studio podcast footage, so one edit serves outreach and the public feed. A filter, not a gate.

**Before pricing any prospect, name what counts as the conversion for them** (booked calls, a trackable link, DMs that reference the content, funnel numbers). If it can't be named, they aren't ready.
